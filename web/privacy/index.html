Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Qibla iPhone app, which shows the Qibla direction using the rhumb line and calculates prayer times, and this website. Effective 21 November 2026.

The short version

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your iPhone's location to work out the Qibla bearing, the distance to the Kaaba and your prayer times. This is calculated entirely on your iPhone. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not saved after you close the app.

You can refuse location access, or turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. You can then choose a city by hand instead.

Compass

The app reads the iPhone's compass heading to turn the dial. The heading stays on the device.

Data kept on your iPhone

The app saves your settings (calculation method, Asr school, minute adjustments and any city you choose) on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network

The app works offline and makes no network requests of its own. Links in the app's About page open this website in Safari.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome through the project's GitHub issues.
