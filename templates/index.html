Qibla in North America

Succinct clarification

The Qibla direction in North America should point towards the East/Southeast, not the Northeast as commonly and incorrectly adopted by the vast majority of mosques today. This is not because of a belief in a flat Earth or a disregard for science, but rather because any calculations must align with both the objectives and the means as defined by Shariah.

According to Islamic scripture and the early consensu of the four authoritative schools of interpretation, the origin of the spherical coordinate system is deemed to be the north pole, not the center of the Earth. The Arabs relied on stars to locate the North Pole and accordingly ascertain the Qibla direction. Allah said: وَعَلَامَاتٍ ۚ وَبِالنَّجْمِ هُمْ يَهْتَدُونَ which means: Allah made clear landmarks on earth (not in earth) and stars (outside earth) for mankind to guide themselves during days and nights.

Therefore, I adhere to the constant bearing of the rhumb line, rather than the initial bearing of the great circle, which entirly disregrads the frame of reference as outlined by Shariah.

It is essential to emphasize that neither great circle nor rhumb line calculations are necessary for determining the Qibla direction in North America. Shariah does not mandate knowledge of geometry for identifying the Qibla direction. However, if someone chooses to use such mathematical constructs, they should ensure that their calculations align with the original Shariah frame of reference.
